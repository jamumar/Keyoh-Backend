Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 April 2026

KEYOH is operated by Dare to Accept Limited. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data.

1. What we collect

We collect your name, email address, and property preferences when you create an account. We also collect information about homes you save, messages you send, and how you use the app.

2. How we use it

We use your data to match you with homes, improve your experience, and send you relevant notifications. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Who we share it with

We share only what is necessary — for example, your first name and verified status with sellers you message. We use trusted third-party services for payments and video hosting.

4. Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to delete your account, or withdraw consent at any time. Contact us at contact@keyoh.co.uk.

5. Cookies

We use cookies to keep you logged in and improve app performance. You can manage cookie preferences in your device settings.

6. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy, email us at contact@keyoh.co.uk or write to Dare to Accept Limited, Nottingham, UK.
