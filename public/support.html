Find answers to common questions below. If you still need help, email our team and we'll get back to you within one business day.

For buyers

How does the matching work?

KEYOH uses your watch history and life signals to find homes that fit your actual life — not just your search filters. The more you watch, the smarter your matches get.

What is a Verified Buyer?

Verified Buyers get priority with sellers, unlimited matches, early access to new listings, and the full top 10 solicitor recommendations. It costs £9.99 per month and you can cancel any time.

How do I arrange a viewing?

Message the seller directly through KEYOH. Viewings are arranged privately between you and the seller. KEYOH does not manage viewings.

Is my data safe?

Yes. We never sell your personal data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

For sellers

How do I list my home?

Tap the seller path from the home screen, follow the listing steps, and publish your home. You can list yourself for free or upgrade your visibility with a boost.

What is a boost?

Boosts increase your listing visibility. SEEN costs £79, TRENDING costs £199, and PRIME costs £499. All boosts are one-off payments and run for 30 days.

Can I hand over to an estate agent?

Yes. At any point you can tap the handover button in your seller dashboard and pass your listing to a KEYOH-partnered agent.

How do offers work?

Only Verified Buyers can submit offers. You will be notified immediately when an offer arrives. You can accept, decline, or counter through KEYOH.

Still need help?

Email us with your account email and a short description of the issue. Our team is based in Nottingham, UK.
